TIPS TO GROW YOUR REVENUE

Five Tricks Grocery Stores Can Learn From Big Retail Chains

TIP 1

The Power of Eye Level

Shelf placement influences what customers notice first. Use prime shelf space strategically.

 

TIP 2

The First Impression Rule

The entrance sets the tone for the shopping experience. Keep it clean, attractive, and easy to navigate.

 

TIP 3

The Power of Three Choices

Group good, better, and premium options to make comparisons easier.

 

TIP 4

The Freshness Effect

Attractive, well-maintained produce displays can improve customers’ perception of the entire store.

 

TIP 5

The Familiarity Advantage

Keep everyday essentials in predictable locations while refreshing promotional displays.
