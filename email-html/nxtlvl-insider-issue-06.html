TIPS TO GROW YOUR REVENUE

5 Ways to Increase Sales at Your Checkout Counter

Your checkout counter isn’t just where customers pay. It’s your last chance to add a few extra dollars to every sale.

TIP 1

Keep It Under $5

Stock small, affordable items like gum, mints, chocolate, and snacks. Low prices make impulse purchases easier.

 

TIP 2

Put Bestsellers at Eye Level

Don’t hide your most tempting products. Place popular grab-and-go items where customers naturally look while waiting to pay.

 

TIP 3

Rotate Products Weekly

Customers stop noticing displays that never change. Rotate seasonal treats, new arrivals, and trending snacks to keep the counter interesting.

 

TIP 4

Make It a Deal

Try offers like “2 for $3” or “Add a Drink for $1.” Simple, easy-to-understand deals can turn one small purchase into two.

 

TIP 5

Use Checkout Space to Test New Products

Not sure whether a new snack or product will sell? Give it a spot at checkout for a week and track the results. It’s a high-visibility area to test customer interest before committing valuable shelf space elsewhere in the store.

 

THE TAKEAWAY

A few extra dollars per customer might seem small, but across hundreds or thousands of transactions, those impulse purchases can add up to thousands of dollars in additional daily revenue.
